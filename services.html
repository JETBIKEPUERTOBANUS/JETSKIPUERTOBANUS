Explore the Mediterranean with Our Jet Ski Excursions

JET SKI

Experience the excitement and freedom of the water with our jet skis.

We depart from Puerto Banús and explore Marbella Bay or sail toward Estepona, offering you a unique adrenaline-filled experience.

Choose Your Adventure Style

  • Free excursion: If you have experience, enjoy without restrictions.
  • With monitor: Perfect for those looking for a safe and guided adventure.

On our excursions, you'll be able to spot dolphins and other marine animals in their natural habitat, making every outing unforgettable.

Plus, some of our 11 motorcycles are equipped with music, adding an extra layer of fun to the experience

BOAT TRIP

Boat Tours – Relax and Enjoy Paradise

If you prefer a more relaxing experience, our boat trips will take you to discover the Marbella coast from a unique perspective.

Relax, enjoy the sun, and admire the beauty of the Mediterranean. You might even see dolphins during the tour, making it a unique experience.
